Hello! I'm Douglas Adams, a visual artist based in California. After majoring in Film and Illustration at Marlboro College, I took an extended break while building a career in software development. I returned to art during the pandemic and have kept it up since! I've been studying figurative, representational, and narrative illustration largely through online instruction. All of my work here is digital, most drawn with Clip Studio Paint on a Wacom tablet.

A few years ago, I also rediscovered my interest in photography and took what I learned from my drawing studies with me. I alternated between digital and film while relearning fundamentals and experimenting with different styles. With film, I shot mostly Tri-X 400 and occasional Portra 400 with a Nikon FM2. For digital, I shoot on a Nikon Z5. In both mediums, I love 50mm and 40mm primes.

This site represents my work as I continue to search for my artistic voice and style. If you'd like to reach me, please send a message to: doug@douglasryanadams.com
